## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Pawanet Planner is the all-in-one companion for nature lovers, conservation enthusiasts, and curious explorers. Whether you spend weekends on the trail or just like learning about the natural world from your couch, Pawanet Planner helps you plan, discover, and reflect on every wild moment.

Built around five focused tools — Biomes, Guide, Journal, Impact, and Lab — the app turns the way you experience nature into something organized, thoughtful, and beautiful.

EXPLORE BIOMES
Browse curated profiles of forests, savannas, wetlands, deserts, reefs, and more. Each biome includes climate notes, key species, conservation status, and the best times to visit.

FIELD GUIDE
A clean, fast guide to the plants and animals you might encounter. Filter by habitat, identify what you saw, and save favorites for later reference.

NATURE JOURNAL
Capture sightings, hikes, and observations the moment they happen. Add notes, dates, and locations to build a personal log that grows with every trip.

IMPACT TRACKER
See your conservation footprint at a glance. Track sustainable habits, volunteer hours, and the ecosystems you've supported over time.

LAB
Hands-on lessons and short activities about ecology, climate, and wildlife biology. Learn at your own pace with bite-sized modules.

WHY PAWANET PLANNER
• Calm, distraction-free design — no ads, no clutter
• Works great for solo hikers, families, students, and educators
• Curated content reviewed for accuracy
• Built for the field: simple, readable, and quick to use
• Lightweight, fast, and respectful of your battery

WHO IT'S FOR
• Hikers and birdwatchers planning their next outing
• Students studying ecology, geography, or biology
• Teachers building lesson plans around biomes and species
• Families looking for a screen-time alternative that gets kids excited about nature
• Anyone who wants to live a little closer to the wild world

PRIVACY
We don't sell your data. Your journal entries and saved favorites stay on your device unless you choose to back them up.

Pawanet Planner is your pocket-sized guide to the world outside your door. Open it before the hike. Open it after the hike. Open it just because.
